\title{A Toy Imperative Language and Its Hoare Logic}
\author{The Interproof demo}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We fix a small imperative language with assignment, sequencing, a conditional
and a loop; give it a big-step operational semantics; state a Hoare logic of
partial correctness over that semantics; and prove the proof system sound.
Everything here is standard and deliberately tiny: the point of the document
is to be read beside the Lean development that formalizes it. The companion
note fixes the notation for stores and frames.
\end{abstract}
